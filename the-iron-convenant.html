The Iron Rules

Our community exists for the elite in spirit, not just in wealth. Infractions are met with zero tolerance.

  • Authenticity is sacred. Fake photos, AI‑generated imagery, or misrepresentation of identity will be discovered and penalised.
  • No solicitation. This is not a marketplace. Promoting external businesses or services will result in immediate expulsion.
  • Respect the sanctity of private communication. Screenshots of conversations or profiles are forbidden and will be pursued legally.
  • Report breaches instantly. Failure to report a violation makes you complicit. Silence is consent to the degradation of our standards.
  • Consequences are final. There is no appeals process. The decision of the Concierge is absolute.
